## Download

1. Open the **[latest release](https://github.com/alwifearzz17/blursafe-releases/releases/latest)** and download **`BlurSafe-Setup-x.y.z.exe`**.
2. Run it. It installs for your Windows user only, no admin needed.
3. Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Updates are automatic:** when a new version comes out, an **Update** button appears in the app's top bar. Click it and BlurSafe updates itself.

## What it does

- **Draw to hide**: rectangle, ellipse or brush, with Gaussian blur, pixelate or a solid block, and a live preview.
- **Lossless**: only the blurred areas change. Everything else, plus EXIF and the colour profile, stays bit-for-bit identical.
- **Offline face AI**: finds every face, labels male / female, auto-blurs male faces, lets you pick faces or blur everyone except the ones you choose.
- **Batch**: open whole folders, apply to all photos, review every face in one grid.
- **Keep or replace originals**: replaced originals go to the Recycle Bin.
- **Private**: everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and BMP.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
